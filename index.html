Plan notes

Schedule

Lifting is stacked with swimming on Mon / Wed / Fri. If recovery feels tough by Friday, shift lift days to Mon / Thu / Sat instead so they're not always paired with back-to-back swim days.

Equipment & progression

  • Dumbbells: start light (5–10 lbs) — the movements are plenty of stimulus at first.
  • Bands: get light / medium / heavy; move up as pull-aparts and rows get easy.
  • Progress every 2–3 weeks: add resistance or 1–2 reps once a movement feels manageable.
  • Track how you feel more than weight moved for the first month — this is about building a base you can progress for years.
  • No known joint limitations. If knees, back, or hips start to bother you, scale back range of motion and let it guide adjustments.

Why this structure

Swimming already provides full-body, joint-friendly cardio, so the three lifting days use full-body sessions with rotating emphasis rather than a traditional split. That keeps a consistent strength stimulus without a 4th or 5th lifting day. The focus is on functional patterns — squat, hinge, push, pull, carry — that translate to daily life.
